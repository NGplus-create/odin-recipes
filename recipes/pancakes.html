Description

This is a simple and easy to make pancake recipe. Perfect for those lazy mornings when you are operating on minimal energy. Try it, you'll love them.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1 1/2 cups Milk
  • 2 Eggs
  • Butter
  • Honey
  • Salt
  • Cinnamon
  • Blueberries

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients together
  2. Mix liquid ingredients in a separate container then add to the flour mixture
  3. Melt butter into pan then add batter
  4. Serve with honey and berries
