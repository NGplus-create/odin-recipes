Description

To no one's surprise, even the tried and trusted can get monotonous over time. That's why it is such a breath of fresh air to have an alternative to the oats staple. A recipe that doesn't deviate much but adds much needed flavour and variety to a golden oldie.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups oats
  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1 cup nuts of choice
  • Honey or sugar
  • Cinnamon

Steps

  1. Add oats to baking tray along with nuts
  2. Add cinnamon, salt and honey on top of oats then mix together
  3. Put baking tray into oven and bake at 180 degrees
  4. Remove when ready and add raisins for sweetened taste
