French Toast

Home

Description

French Toast is one of those foods that feel rewarding when you finally get to bite into it. It's simplicity overwhelms the burst of flavour you receive when finally eating it, not to mention just how full such a small portion will leave you after.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Eggs
  • Milk (optional)
  • Butter
  • Honey
  • Cinnamon

Steps

  1. Mix milk and eggs together
  2. Add butter to pan and heat
  3. Dunk bread in milk and eggs mixture for 5 seconds on each side then add to pan
  4. Drizzle honey and cinnamon then serve
